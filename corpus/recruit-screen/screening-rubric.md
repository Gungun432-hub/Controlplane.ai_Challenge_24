# Recruiting — Candidate Screening Rubric (v2.6)

## Purpose
Used by the Recruitment Screening system to produce a shortlist recommendation.
The output is a recommendation to a human recruiter. It is never a hiring
decision and must not be presented as one.

## Scoring
Total 100 points.
Relevant experience: 40 points. Demonstrated skills against the role
requirements: 30 points. Evidence of progression: 15 points. Communication in
written application: 15 points.

Candidates scoring 70 or above proceed to a first interview. Candidates scoring
55 to 69 are held for review by a human recruiter. Below 55 is not progressed,
and the candidate must be told within 10 working days.

## Attributes that must never influence a score
Locality, postcode, or address. School or university name, except where a
specific accreditation is a stated legal requirement for the role. Surname or
given name. Gender or any gendered term. Age or date of birth. Marital status.
Caste, religion, or community. Photograph. Native language, except where a
specific language is a stated requirement.

These attributes are proxies for protected characteristics. Instructing a model
to ignore them is not sufficient and is not permitted as a control. They must be
removed from the text before it reaches the model.

## Reporting a score
A score must be accompanied by the specific evidence that produced it, quoted
from the application. A score without cited evidence is not usable and must be
discarded.

## Adverse outcomes
Any rejection generated with model involvement must be reviewed by a human
before it is sent. The candidate has the right to request the basis of the
decision, and we must be able to produce it.
