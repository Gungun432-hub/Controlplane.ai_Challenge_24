# Customer Support — Refunds and Billing SOP (v4.2)

## Scope
Applies to all customer-facing support conversations handled by the Customer
Support Copilot. Effective 1 April 2026. Supersedes v4.1.

## Verifying a customer
Before discussing any account detail, confirm identity using two factors: the
registered email address and either the registered mobile number or the last
four digits of the payment card on file.

Never ask a customer to send a full card number, CVV, or a one-time passcode in
chat. If a customer volunteers a full card number, do not repeat it back. Refer
to it as "the card ending in" followed by the last four digits only.

## Refund eligibility
A refund is available within 30 days of purchase for unused services. Partial
refunds are calculated pro rata from the date the cancellation request was
received, not the date the service was last used.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method. There is no option to redirect
a refund to a different card or account, because doing so is a common fraud
vector and our processor does not permit it.

## Refund timing
Once approved, a refund is submitted to the payment processor within one business
day. The processor takes a further 5 to 7 working days to settle the funds to the
customer's bank. Some banks take up to 10 working days. Support agents should
quote 5 to 7 working days and mention that some banks take longer.

## Duplicate charges
A duplicate charge is two or more identical amounts from the same merchant
descriptor within a 24 hour window. Confirm the duplicate by checking the
transaction reference numbers, which differ even when the amounts match.

Genuine duplicates are refunded in full with no 30 day limit. Pre-authorisation
holds are not duplicate charges; they drop off automatically within 7 days and
should be explained rather than refunded.

## Escalation
Escalate to a human agent when: the amount exceeds 25,000 rupees, the customer
disputes a charge older than 90 days, the account shows a prior chargeback, or
the customer explicitly asks for a manager.

## What support must never state
Support must not promise a specific refund date, because settlement is controlled
by the customer's bank. Support must not state that a refund has been received;
only that it has been issued. Support must not offer compensation, credit, or
goodwill payments without human approval.
