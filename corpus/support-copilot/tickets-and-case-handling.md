# Customer Support — Tickets and Case Handling (v3.1)

## Ticket states
A ticket moves through five states: **new**, **open**, **in progress**,
**waiting on customer**, and **resolved**.

A ticket is **open** from the moment it is raised until an agent picks it up.
It moves to **in progress** when an agent or the copilot is actively working it.
It moves to **waiting on customer** when we have asked the customer for
information and cannot proceed without it.

## Telling a customer the status of their ticket
When a customer asks about the status of an open ticket, state the current state,
what was last done, and what happens next. Do not estimate a resolution time
unless the ticket type has a published target.

If the ticket is **waiting on customer**, say so plainly and repeat exactly what
is needed. The most common cause of a slow case is a request for information the
customer never saw.

An open ticket that has had no activity for 48 hours is automatically flagged for
a human agent to review.

## Resolution targets by ticket type
Billing query: 1 working day. Delivery query: 1 working day.
Refund request: 2 working days to a decision, then the published refund timing.
Product fault: 3 working days. Complaint: 5 working days, always handled by a
human.

These are targets for a first substantive response and a decision, not for money
to arrive in a customer's account.

## Reopening
A resolved ticket can be reopened by the customer within 14 days and keeps its
original reference. After 14 days a new ticket is raised and linked to the old
one. Never tell a customer that a closed ticket cannot be revisited.

## Order and account questions
Order status, delivery window, and tracking are read from the order system of
record. If the system of record has no update, say that there is no update rather
than describing what usually happens.

Account balance, stored payment methods, and address changes are handled in the
account system. The copilot may read them and may not change them; any change is
handed to a human agent.
