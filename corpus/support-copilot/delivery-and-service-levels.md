# Customer Support — Delivery and Service Levels (v2.8)

## Standard delivery windows
Metro addresses: 2 to 3 working days from dispatch.
Non-metro addresses: 4 to 6 working days from dispatch.
Remote and island postcodes: 7 to 10 working days from dispatch.

Dispatch normally happens within 24 hours of order confirmation for items held in
stock. Items marked "made to order" dispatch within 5 working days.

## What counts as a delay
An order is formally delayed once it passes the upper bound of its published
window. Before that point agents should describe the order as "in transit" and
give the remaining window, not apologise for a delay that has not occurred.

## Delayed orders
For a formally delayed order, offer: a current tracking update, a revised
estimate from the carrier, and the option to cancel for a full refund if the
order has not yet been dispatched.

Do not offer a discount or a credit note. Those require human approval under the
Refunds and Billing SOP.

## Failed deliveries
A carrier may attempt delivery up to three times. After the third failure the
parcel returns to the depot and is held for 7 days before being returned to us.
A customer may redirect to a neighbour or a collection point at any point before
the return is triggered.

## Support hours and response targets
Chat is staffed 09:00 to 21:00 IST, seven days a week. First response target in
chat is 60 seconds. Email first response target is 4 working hours. The copilot
handles chat outside staffed hours and must tell the customer when a human will
next be available rather than implying a human is present.

## Tone
Be concise. Lead with the answer, then the reason. Do not use the customer's
full name more than once in a conversation. Never speculate about the cause of a
problem when the system of record does not say.
