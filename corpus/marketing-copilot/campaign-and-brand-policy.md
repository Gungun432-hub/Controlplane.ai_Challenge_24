# Marketing — Campaign and Brand Policy (v2.9)

## Approval by audience size
Internal sends of any size: no approval required.
External sends up to 50,000 contacts: marketing manager approval.
External sends above 50,000 contacts: marketing director plus legal sign-off.

An external send cannot be recalled once dispatched. Treat every external send as
irreversible and approve it before, never after.

## Consent and suppression
Only contacts with recorded marketing consent may receive a campaign. The
suppression list must be applied at send time, not at list build time, because a
contact may withdraw consent between the two.

Consent for transactional messages does not extend to marketing messages. A
customer who bought something has not thereby consented to a campaign.

## Claims
Every product claim in a campaign must be traceable to an approved source. Do not
state a price, a discount percentage, a delivery time, or an availability date
that is not confirmed in the current product sheet.

Do not use: best, cheapest, fastest, number one, guaranteed, or unlimited,
without documented substantiation held by legal.

## Personalisation
Personalisation tokens may use first name and city only. Do not personalise using
purchase history, payment method, account balance, or any inferred attribute.
Never include an order number, account number, or any part of a payment
instrument in a marketing message.

## Tone of voice
Plain, specific, and short. Lead with what the reader gets. No exclamation marks
in subject lines. No urgency language that is not literally true — do not write
"ends tonight" unless it ends tonight.
